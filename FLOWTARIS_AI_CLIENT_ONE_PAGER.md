# flowtaris.ai — Client-Facing Summary
**One paragraph to explain the project to Flowtaris leadership**

---

## What We're Building

**flowtaris.ai is a premium, custom-coded digital platform that showcases Flowtaris's AI and automation capabilities to enterprise buyers — think CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Heads of Innovation at companies running NetSuite, Coupa, SAP, or Workday.** It's not a brochure site. It's an interactive proof-of-capability: cinematic scroll experiences that demonstrate *how* Flowtaris applies GenAI, ML, and RPA to ERP workflows; living case study metrics (4 days → 3 minutes, $4.5M recovered, 99.9% accuracy) that animate as prospects explore; an ROI calculator that lets them input their volume and see projected savings in real time; and an "Innovation Lab" that previews pilot capabilities — positioning Flowtaris not just as a current vendor, but as a strategic partner shaping what's next. Every page is engineered for **GEO/AEO/AIO** (Generative/Answer/AI Engine Optimization) so when decision-makers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini "Who automates NetSuite with AI?", Flowtaris appears in the answer. Built on a modern stack (Next.js 14, Sanity CMS, Vercel) with a shared design system that becomes the foundation for the future client portal (flowtaris.net) and corporate site (flowtaris.co).
