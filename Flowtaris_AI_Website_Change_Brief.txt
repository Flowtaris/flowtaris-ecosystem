1. Executive action plan
The website has a strong visual concept, but its current public experience creates significant conversion, credibility, legal, and reputational risk. The priority is to make every conversion path truthful and functional before expanding content or traffic acquisition.
RECOMMENDED SEQUENCE  Stabilize links and legal pages first; verify identity and claims second; correct calculators and content consistency third; finish SEO, accessibility, and responsive QA last.

4. Calculator and assessment changes
The tools operate, but their outputs can undermine trust if presented as decision-grade financial analysis without transparent assumptions and guardrails.
ComponentObserved problemRequired replacement/updateAssessment roadmapGenerated recommendations included 519-, 726-, and 344-month paybacks, yet presented them as recommended strategic initiatives.Add viability rules: reject or clearly flag recommendations above an approved payback threshold; explain why an opportunity is not recommended.Assessment stateThe assessment reopened at step 6 with prior values; industry query variants did not clearly restart or personalize the flow.Provide “Start new assessment / Reset” control, disclose local persistence, and map query parameters deterministically.Assessment copyMechanical labels such as “Genai Doc Intelligence” and “Conversational Erp.”Use approved display-name mapping rather than slug-to-title conversion.ROI modelDefault result showed $1.576M annual savings and 0.6-month payback while FAQ says typical payback is 3-8 months.Reconcile formula, implementation cost, ramp time, confidence intervals, and FAQ; show low/base/high scenarios.ROI benchmarks“LIVE MARKET BENCHMARKS (AUG 2026)” and “18.2M SIM units” have no citations.Replace “live” with a dated, sourced benchmark statement; publish methodology and distinguish simulated from production data.Cost of Inaction$2.642M three-year leakage appears precise without sufficient methodology.Expose formulas, editable assumptions, source dates, scenario sensitivity, exclusions, and a clear “illustrative estimate” disclaimer.Email/exportExport and report flows request email.Do not enable until Privacy, consent, retention, delivery endpoint, and security testing pass.
5. SEO, content quality, accessibility, and responsive changes
* Replace duplicated titles such as “... | Flowtaris AI | Flowtaris AI” with one unique, page-specific title.
* Use page-specific canonicals. Do not canonicalize unrelated pages to https://flowtaris.ai/ while that domain is parked.
* Add one meaningful H1 to Assessment and ROI Calculator.
* Set error pages to noindex and ensure 404s return the correct HTTP status rather than only rendering “404” text.
* Render headings as text with layout styling; remove literal “<br/>” from Insights, Platforms, About, and Contact headings.
* Remove visible development residue such as “X: 0.0deg Y: 0.0deg.”
* Replace internal-facing copy such as “GEO-optimized for AI search visibility” with customer-facing editorial positioning.
* Add accessible names to mobile icon controls; verify focus order, keyboard operation, visible focus, labels, contrast, and error messaging.
* Re-test Contact from a clean mobile load; its heading appeared displaced above the viewport during the responsive pass.
* Add an approved copyright notice and legal entity name in the footer.
* Standardize interior/footer navigation so critical legal and contact links appear consistently.

10. Audit scope and limitations
The source website was reviewed in a live browser. Unique discovered HTTP(S) destinations were opened; repeated instances of shared navigation links were inventoried by destination rather than clicked individually. Telephone and mailto links were inspected but not launched because they would open local communications applications. Forms requesting personal information were not submitted. Representative mobile checks were performed at approximately 390 x 844; this does not replace full physical-device, assistive-technology, security, performance, or legal testing.
Status language in this brief reflects observed browser behavior on 27 August 2026. Company, funding, customer, team, investor, certification, and performance statements are flagged for verification; this brief does not independently assert that those claims are true or false.
